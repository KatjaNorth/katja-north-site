About

Jekaterina Rogaten

Hi, I’m Jekaterina Rogaten — an AI video creator based in Denmark, working under the brand Katja North.

I turn ideas into cinematic short films, commercials and social media content with generative AI. I handle the whole process: concept, script, storyboard, generation, voice, music and final edit.

I’m creative by nature, with a wealth of life experience behind me. It has given me a trained eye and a strong sense of taste, and both go into every frame I make.

My work ranges from cinematic commercials and musicals to animated films, photorealistic video and fairy tales that blend real footage with AI. I also create cinematic historical films, where I bring old photographs of real people to life, and 3D work for projects that need full control over the scene and precision in every detail.

Tools I use include Seedance, Higgsfield, Kling, Midjourney, Suno, Claude, ChatGPT and CapCut — but that’s only part of the list. I learn fast, understand the principles behind these tools, and grow along with them as they evolve.

I speak Danish, English and Russian, and I’m open to both in-house roles and freelance projects.
