Privacy policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026

This page explains which personal data this website handles, why, and what rights you have. In short: the site sets no cookies and uses no tracking, and I only receive the details you send me yourself through the contact form.

Who is responsible for your data

The data controller is Jekaterina Rogaten, an AI video creator based in Denmark, working under the brand Katja North.

E-mail: jekaterinaro691 [at] gmail.com

What I collect and why

When you send a message through the contact form, I receive your name, your e-mail address, the subject you chose and the text of your message.

I use these details only to reply to you and, if we agree to work together, to handle the project. I do not use them for newsletters or advertising, and I do not sell or share them for marketing.

The legal basis is my legitimate interest in answering enquiries (GDPR Article 6(1)(f)) and, when your message concerns a job offer or a project, taking steps at your request before entering into a contract (Article 6(1)(b)).

You are not obliged to give me these details, but without a name and an e-mail address I cannot reply to you.

Who else handles the data

  • Formspree (Formspree, Inc., USA) receives the contact form and forwards your message to me. It acts as my data processor.
  • Google (Gmail) stores the message in my e-mail inbox.
  • GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., USA) hosts this website. Like any web host, it processes your IP address and technical request data when you open a page, for operation and security.

Formspree, Google and GitHub are based in the USA, so your data may be transferred outside the EU/EEA. Each of them describes the safeguards it uses for such transfers in its own privacy policy:

  • formspree.io/legal/privacy-policy
  • policies.google.com/privacy
  • GitHub General Privacy Statement

Videos, cookies and tracking

This website sets no cookies and uses no analytics or advertising trackers. Fonts and images are served from the site itself.

Videos are hosted on YouTube. Nothing is loaded from YouTube until you press play on a video. When you do, the player is loaded from youtube-nocookie.com (YouTube’s privacy-enhanced mode), and Google receives your IP address and technical data and may store information on your device. Google’s privacy policy then applies.

The buttons to LinkedIn, YouTube, Instagram and Telegram are ordinary links. Those services receive data only if you follow the link.

How long I keep it

I keep messages for as long as it takes to deal with your enquiry and any work that follows, and normally delete them no later than 12 months after our last contact. Documents I am required by law to keep, such as invoices, are kept for the period the law prescribes.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to ask for access to your data, to have it corrected or deleted, to have its processing restricted, to object to the processing, and to receive your data in a portable format. To use any of these rights, write to me at the e-mail address above. I will answer within one month.

No automated decisions or profiling are made on the basis of your data.

If you are unhappy with how I handle your data, you can complain to the Danish Data Protection Agency (Datatilsynet): datatilsynet.dk

Changes to this policy

If the way I handle data changes, I will update this page and the date at the top.
